Maxillofacial Surgery is the specialty of surgery / dentistry concerned with the diagnosis and treatment of diseases affecting the mouth, jaws, face and neck. Facial surgery is complex and unique as there is not only a physical component to the surgery but also a psychological and social component involved. Extensive hands-on experience and a highly skilled team is vital for best results.

At Shanker Dental and Craniofacial Centre, we have a very experienced team of doctors from various medical and dental specialties including anaesthetics, ENT, neurosurgery, plastic surgery, ophthalmology, orthodontics, prosthetics and endodontics to deal with complex problems of the face. We also have other specialties like medicine and cardiology on call if needed. Good nursing care is also given a lot of importance to provide the best care to our patients. Every surgery is extensively planned to provide the best results to our patients.

The scope of the specialty

  • Craniofacial Surgery
  • Facial Deformity Corrective Surgery (Orthognathic)
  • Facial Trauma Surgery
  • Cleft Lip and Palate Surgery
  • Temporomandibular (TMJ: Jaw Joint) Surgery
  • Dental and Facial Implants
  • Pathology of the Head and Neck: Benign and Malignant
  • Dentoalveolar surgery

We do the entire range of maxillofacial procedures at Shanker Dental and Craniofacial Centre and our results are comparable to the best centres in the world.
